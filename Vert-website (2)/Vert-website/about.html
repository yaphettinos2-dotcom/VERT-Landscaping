Who we are

VERT Landscaping was established in Addis Ababa and brings more than 7 years of practical experience to landscape design, construction, and garden maintenance. Our work combines modern aesthetics with practical site decisions that hold up over time.

What we value

  • Clear design thinking that fits the site and the client
  • Implementation quality that respects detail and finish
  • Planting strategies that support long-term health
  • Outdoor spaces that feel calm, useful, and complete
Our Approach

Landscape work that balances beauty, function, and long-term care.

We treat each project as a full environment. That means considering movement, planting structure, drainage, material character, maintenance, and how the space should feel once people begin living with it.

Design philosophy

  • Balanced layouts with clear circulation and rhythm
  • Planting that adds texture, shade, softness, and identity
  • Hardscape choices that feel durable and context-aware
  • Landscape decisions shaped by site conditions and real use

Working process

  • Site study and project understanding
  • Concept development and planning
  • Implementation with quality control
  • Maintenance thinking that protects the final result
